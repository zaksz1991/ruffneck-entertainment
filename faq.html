Frequently asked questions

Short answers about services, delivery and how to work with us.

What does RuffNeck Entertainment do?

We help businesses and professionals improve operations, adopt practical AI, and grow through reliable digital systems — from training and automation to IT support, content, websites, and career documents.

Where are you based?

Kano, Nigeria, with remote delivery across Nigeria, Africa, and selected international markets.

How do I start?

Book a discovery call, request a quote on the website, or message us on WhatsApp. For instant downloads, use the Store.

Do you publish fixed prices?

Some Store products show prices at checkout. Custom services are quoted after discovery because scope, complexity, timeline and support needs vary.

How are digital products delivered?

Electronically after successful payment — usually immediately by download link or email. See the Delivery Policy.

Can I get a refund on a PDF?

Digital goods are generally final after delivery. If payment succeeded but the file is missing or corrupt, contact us with your payment reference.

Do you guarantee search rankings or sales?

No. We deliver agreed work and practical guidance. Market results depend on many factors outside a single project.

Is my form data private?

We use enquiry and lead-magnet data to respond and deliver resources. See the Privacy Policy for retention and requests.
