Community Impact · Digital Skills

Building Africa’s next generation of digital professionals.

The RuffNeck Digital Empowerment Programme equips women and young people with practical AI, digital-business, technology and entrepreneurship skills for employment, freelancing and self-employment.

Apply for the next cohort Partner or sponsor the programme
Three practical pathways

Choose a track that matches your goals.

Each pathway combines practical training, guided assignments, portfolio development, mentorship and a clear next step.

Track 1

Women in Digital Business

For women starting, improving or growing a digital business, seeking freelance opportunities or building remote-work skills.

  • AI-assisted content and social media
  • Canva, branding and digital products
  • WhatsApp Business and client support
  • Freelancing, pricing and operations

Outcome: A practical digital-business plan, sample content, an online profile and a clear next earning step.

Apply for this track
Track 2

Youth AI and Tech Skills

A beginner-friendly pathway for young people exploring AI, technology and remote-work careers.

  • AI literacy and responsible AI
  • Prompt engineering and research
  • Data annotation and technical content
  • Portfolio, CV and remote readiness

Outcome: Foundational technology skills, practical projects and portfolio evidence for internships, freelancing or further training.

Apply for this track
Track 3

Digital Entrepreneurship

A shared pathway for women and young people turning skills into services, products or sustainable digital opportunities.

  • Offer design and market problem fit
  • Brand identity and online presence
  • First customers through WhatsApp and social media
  • Basic finances and responsible AI use

Outcome: A simple market-ready digital offer and a practical plan for reaching first customers.

Apply for this track
Support beyond training

Training continues after the workshop.

Participants receive structured support to apply what they learn, build evidence of capability and prepare for their next opportunity.

Monthly community sessions

Peer accountability, questions and answers, progress check-ins and practical learning conversations.

Mentor office hours

Feedback on projects, portfolios, business ideas and professional direction.

Career and portfolio support

CV and LinkedIn reviews, showcase days, pathway information and practical application guidance.
