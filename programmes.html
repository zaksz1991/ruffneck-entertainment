Community Impact · RDEP

Building Africa’s Next Generation of Digital Professionals

The RuffNeck Digital Empowerment Programme equips women and young people with practical AI, digital-business, technology, and entrepreneurship skills for employment, freelancing, and self-employment.

Apply for the Next Cohort Partner or Sponsor the Programme

We believe access to practical digital skills should not depend on location, gender, background, or formal employment status. Through training, mentorship, portfolio development, and partnerships, RuffNeck helps participants move from interest to practical capability.

Programme tracks

Three practical pathways under one flagship community-impact initiative — structured cohorts, measurable outcomes, and continued mentorship.

Track 1

Women in Digital Business

For women starting, improving, or growing a digital business — or seeking freelance and remote opportunities.

  • AI-assisted content & social media
  • Canva, branding & digital products
  • WhatsApp Business & client support
  • Freelancing, pricing & operations

Outcome: A practical digital-business plan, sample content, a professional online profile, and a clear next earning step.

Apply — Women track
Track 2

Youth AI & Tech Skills

Beginner-friendly pathway for young people exploring AI, tech, and remote-work careers.

  • AI literacy & responsible AI
  • Prompt engineering & research
  • Data annotation & tech content
  • Portfolio, CV & remote readiness

Outcome: Foundational tech skills, practical projects, and portfolio evidence for internships, freelancing, or further training.

Apply — Youth track
Track 3

Digital Entrepreneurship

Shared track for women and youth turning skills into services or products.

  • Offer design & market problem fit
  • Brand identity & online presence
  • First customers via WhatsApp & social
  • Basic finances & responsible AI use

Outcome: A simple market-ready digital offer and a practical plan to reach first customers.

Apply — Entrepreneurship

Six-week cohort structure

Example format: online, on-site, or blended · 25–50 participants · 2 sessions/week · 90–120 minutes · final showcase.

WeekThemePractical output
1Digital & AI foundationsSkills audit & learning plan
2AI tools & responsible usePrompt library & checklist
3Content, branding & communicationBrand profile & sample content
4Business, freelancing & remote workService offer or career pathway
5Portfolio & digital presenceCV, LinkedIn, portfolio, or business page
6Showcase & next stepsFinal project, presentation & action plan

Participants receive structured training, guided assignments, portfolio-ready projects, mentorship, community access, and a certificate of participation where applicable. We offer career guidance and pathway support — not guaranteed job placement.
