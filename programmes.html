Building Africa's Next Generation of Digital Professionals

The RuffNeck Digital Empowerment Programme equips women and young people with practical AI, digital-business, technology and entrepreneurship skills for employment, freelancing and self-employment.

Track 1

  • Canva, branding and digital products

Track 2

Youth AI & Tech Skills

A beginner-friendly pathway for young people exploring AI, technology and remote-work careers.

Ideal for: Students, graduates and young people building their first practical technology portfolio.

  • AI literacy and responsible AI
  • Prompt engineering and research
  • Data annotation and technology content
  • Portfolio, CV and remote-work readiness

Track 3

Digital Entrepreneurship

A shared track for women and youth turning their skills into services or digital products.

Ideal for: Participants with an idea who want to create a clear offer and find their first customers.

  • Offer design and market problem fit
  • Brand identity and online presence
  • First customers through WhatsApp and social media

Six-week cohort structure

Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous AI experience?

No. The programme includes beginner-friendly foundations. Participants should be willing to practise between sessions.

Does the programme guarantee employment?

No. The programme provides training, practical projects, mentorship and pathway guidance. Employment, internships and income are not guaranteed.

Will I receive a certificate?

Participants may receive a certificate of participation where applicable, subject to the programme requirements.

What device do I need?

A smartphone with internet access may be enough for some sessions. A computer is recommended for assignments and portfolio work.

Is the programme online or physical?

Delivery may be online, on-site or blended depending on the cohort, partners and available facilities.

Is the programme free?

Programme availability, funding and participant requirements may vary by cohort. Submit an application or contact RuffNeck for the current details.
