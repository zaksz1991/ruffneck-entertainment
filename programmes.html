RuffNeck Digital Empowerment Programme (RDEP)

Practical digital skills, AI literacy and professional readiness for learners and partners who want usable skills—not theory-only sessions.

Track A · Digital foundations

Computer basics, online safety, Office/Google tools, and confident day-to-day digital use.

Track B · AI literacy

Responsible AI use, prompt practice for real tasks, and quality-checking AI outputs.

Track C · Professional readiness

CV basics, LinkedIn hygiene, remote work habits, and portfolio-style evidence of skills.

Example 6-week cohort shape

WeekFocus
1Orientation, goals, tool setup
2Core digital skills practice
3AI literacy and responsible use
4Applied tasks (content, data or admin)
5Professional profiles and presentation
6Review, feedback, next-step pathways

Cohort dates, delivery mode (online/hybrid), and selection criteria are confirmed per intake. Completing a track does not guarantee employment.
