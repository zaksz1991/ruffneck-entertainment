Privacy Policy

RuffNeck Entertainment · Last updated: 26 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RuffNeck Entertainment (“we”, “us”) collects and uses information when you use our website, forms, WhatsApp contact, email, and digital product purchases.

Who we are

RuffNeck Entertainment is a digital services business registered in Nigeria (CAC Reg. No. 3770977), operating from Kano, with remote delivery to clients in Nigeria, Africa, and selected international markets.

Information we collect

  • Contact and enquiry data: name, email, phone (if provided), message content, service interest.
  • Lead magnet data: name and email when you request the free AI prompts guide or similar resources.
  • Purchase data: information needed to process payment and deliver digital products (handled in part by payment providers such as Flutterwave).
  • Technical data: basic server or analytics logs if enabled (for example pages visited, approximate location derived from IP, device type).
  • WhatsApp/email correspondence: content you send us while discussing a project.

Why we collect it

  • Respond to enquiries and provide quotes
  • Deliver free guides and digital products
  • Provide contracted services
  • Improve the website and prevent abuse
  • Meet legal and accounting obligations

Legal bases (where applicable)

We process data because you request a service or download, because we have a contract or pre-contract steps, because we have a legitimate interest in operating a secure business website, or where you have given consent (for example optional marketing emails).

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Payment processors (for example Flutterwave) to complete purchases
  • Email or form infrastructure used to deliver messages and files
  • Professional advisers or authorities when required by law

Retention

  • Enquiry messages: typically up to 24 months unless a client relationship continues
  • Lead magnet emails: until you ask to be removed, or until the list is refreshed
  • Purchase records: as required for tax and consumer rules

Your choices

  • Request access, correction, or deletion of personal data we hold
  • Ask to stop marketing emails (service messages about an order may still be sent)
  • Contact us using the details below

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures. No method of transmission is 100% secure. Do not send passwords or payment card numbers by email or WhatsApp.

Children

Our services are aimed at businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children for marketing.

International transfers

Tools we use may process data on servers outside Nigeria. By using the site and forms, you understand that your information may be processed in other countries with different data laws.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
