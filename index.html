About RuffNeck

Built on operations. Driven by AI.

RuffNeck Entertainment is a multidisciplinary digital services company based in Kano, Nigeria. We support businesses, professionals, organisations and communities with practical digital systems, operational improvement, AI adoption and growth support.

Our approach is deliberately practical: understand the real problem, design an appropriate solution, explain it clearly and deliver work that people can continue using.

  • Mission: empower businesses and communities through technology and operational excellence.
  • Vision: become a trusted AI and digital transformation partner from Africa.
  • Values: integrity, excellence, collaboration, innovation and continuous learning.
