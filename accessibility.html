Accessibility statement

RuffNeck Entertainment aims to make this website usable by as many people as possible, including people who use keyboards, screen readers, and mobile devices.

Measures we take

  • Semantic headings and landmarks where practical
  • Skip links on major pages
  • Visible focus styles
  • Form labels on key forms
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images where provided
  • Support for reduced-motion preferences on the homepage

Known limitations

Some older pages or third-party embeds (maps, payment widgets, translation tools) may not fully meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. We improve pages progressively.
