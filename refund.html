Refund and Cancellation Policy

RuffNeck Entertainment · Last updated: 26 August 2026

This policy covers digital product purchases and professional service engagements with RuffNeck Entertainment.

Digital products (guides, templates, PDFs)

  • Because products are delivered electronically and can be copied, all sales are generally final after the download link or file has been provided.
  • If payment succeeded but you did not receive the file, contact us within 48 hours with your payment reference. We will re-send the download or confirm delivery.
  • If a file is corrupt or incomplete due to our error, we will replace it.
  • Change of mind, or buying the wrong item, does not normally qualify for a refund once delivery has occurred.

Professional services

  • Deposits may be non-refundable once work has started, as stated on the invoice or proposal.
  • If we cancel a project before delivery, you will receive a fair refund of amounts paid for undelivered work.
  • If you cancel after work has started, fees for completed work and committed third-party costs are payable.
  • Retainer terms (notice period and unused hours) are defined in the retainer agreement.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before opening a dispute with your bank. Many delivery issues can be resolved by re-sending files or clarifying scope.
